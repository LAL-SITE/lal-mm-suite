# Retainer Transmittal — Content Spec
lane: client
sources: 1 distinct matter (negative finding only) + 1 firm-agnostic master template
harvested: 2026-07-29

## When this fires

Retainer agreement has been countersigned by the firm AND the initial payment has
been deposited to trust. Sent within 4 hours of countersignature and trust
deposit — the source is explicit that speed here signals the firm is organized.
The executed retainer PDF is always attached.

This is the FIRST substantive message in the sequence. It precedes the welcome /
engagement message, which follows within 24 hours.

**Integrity caveat carried from source:** this master carries the internal note
"DRAFT FROM MASTER STANDARD — no direct case-file source extracted. Compare to
firm's current retainer transmittal practice and revise before final approval."
The language below is therefore firm-authored standard, not observed sent
correspondence. Buyers must not treat it as battle-tested.

**Second-pass finding — the master describes a document the firm does not appear
to send.** A sweep of the source firm's sent client correspondence found no
firm-drafted retainer transmittal in any matter. What one matter's correspondence
contained instead was an automated notification generated by the firm's
e-signature platform, with a subject in the form:

> {{AGREEMENT_TYPE_AND_YEAR}} between {{FIRM_NAME}}, {{CLIENT_NAME}} and
> {{ROLE_APPROVER}} is Signed and Filed

That is a system notice, not correspondence. It confirms execution to the file;
it does not confirm receipt to the client, state scope, route questions, set the
first-seven-days sequence, or attach an executed copy under firm cover.

The practical implication for the buyer: the gap this document is meant to fill
is real, and in the source practice it is being filled by a platform
notification. A buyer adopting this spec is adopting a document the source firm
has specified but not operationalized. That is a legitimate reason to ship it —
the reasoning in the master is sound — but it must not be represented as
harvested practice.

## Required structure

1. Subject line
2. Salutation — first name only
3. Thanks + receipt confirmation (signed agreement AND payment, both named)
4. "Matter is now officially open" statement
5. WHAT YOU HAVE ENGAGED US TO DO — scope, fee structure, out-of-scope carve-outs
6. Where the executed copy lives (attached AND in portal)
7. WHAT HAPPENS NOW — time-boxed first-7-days sequence
8. YOUR TEAM — named roles with contact routing
9. BILLING — trust credit, statement cadence, who to ask
10. NEXT STEP — single instruction, explicitly low-effort
11. Signature block — attorney signs

## Subject line bank

Observed, tokenized:

- `Your Signed Retainer — Confirmation and What Happens Next`

Only one subject line exists in the source library for this type. Pattern matches
the firm's house style: `<Noun phrase naming the artifact> — <what the reader
gets>`, em dash separator.

## Opening paragraph bank

**Situation: standard full-representation engagement, retainer countersigned and
trust funded**
> {{CLIENT_NAME}},
> Thank you for choosing {{FIRM_NAME}}. We have received your signed retainer
> agreement and your initial payment. Your matter is now officially open with our
> firm.

No alternate opening exists in the source. The firm confirms both the signature

fact in its own sentence.

## Body modules

### MODULE: scope-and-carveouts
Scope, fee structure, and out-of-scope items appear together as a single block.
The out-of-scope list is stated affirmatively, not buried.

> WHAT YOU HAVE ENGAGED US TO DO
> Your retainer covers: [CONFIRM LOCALLY: scope summary copied verbatim from the
> executed agreement]. The fee arrangement is: [CONFIRM LOCALLY: flat fee or
> hourly structure as executed]. Items outside this scope ([CONFIRM LOCALLY:
> common out-of-scope items for this matter type — the source names contempt
> actions, appeals, and post-judgment modifications]) would require a separate
> scope addendum.

### MODULE: executed-copy-location
> A copy of your signed retainer is attached to this email and is also saved in
> your client portal under Documents.

### MODULE: first-7-days-timeline
Three time-boxed commitments. Each states an interval, an actor, and a
deliverable.

> WHAT HAPPENS NOW — YOUR FIRST 7 DAYS
> 1. Within 24 hours: We will send you a separate Welcome message with your intake
>    form and {{CLIENT_UPLOAD_TOOL}} upload links.
> 2. Within 48 hours: {{ROLE_DRAFTER}} will reach out through the
>    portal to introduce themselves and confirm your portal access.
> 3. Within 5 business days: Your attorney will review your file and either
>    schedule an intake call with you, or send your first substantive document for
>    review.

### MODULE: your-team
Routing is explicit — the client is told which human owns which question type, so
billing questions do not land on the attorney.

> YOUR TEAM
> • Attorney: {{ATTORNEY_NAME}}
> • Day-to-day contact: {{ROLE_DRAFTER}}
> • {{ROLE_INTAKE}} (scheduling, billing questions)
> • Main office: {{FIRM_PHONE}}

### MODULE: billing-and-trust
> BILLING
> Your trust account has been credited with your initial payment. You will receive
> monthly statements through the portal showing time entries, costs, and any draws
> against trust. If you have questions about billing, contact {{ROLE_INTAKE}} at
> {{FIRM_EMAIL}}.

Dollar figures appear in the source as a fill-in. Do not populate until the trust
deposit has cleared.

### MODULE: next-step-do-nothing
Distinctive move: the firm explicitly relieves the client of any immediate task.

> NEXT STEP
> Watch for our Welcome message within 24 hours. Until then, you do not need to do
> anything.

## Closing / next-steps bank

- `We are looking forward to working with you.`
- `Until then, you do not need to do anything.` — the deliberate zero-action
  close, paired with the 24-hour promise.

The library contains no alternate close for this type.

## Signature block

Attorney signs the retainer transmittal. (Contrast: {{ROLE_DRAFTER}} signs the
welcome message.)

> Sincerely,
>
> {{ATTORNEY_NAME}}
> {{FIRM_NAME}}

## Voice notes

- Confirms receipt of BOTH the signature and the money in one sentence, then
  states matter status separately. Never leaves payment receipt implied.
- Time-boxes every forward commitment with a concrete interval — "within 24
  hours," "within 48 hours," "within 5 business days." No vague "shortly" or "in
  the coming days."
- Names the out-of-scope items rather than gesturing at limits. Scope discipline
  is set on day one, in the client's own copy.
- Routes questions by type to a named role, which keeps billing traffic off the
  attorney.
- Ends by removing client burden. The firm asks for nothing in this message.
- Short paragraphs, ALL-CAPS section headers, numbered list only for the timeline.
- Formal register relative to the welcome message: "Sincerely," attorney
  signature, "Thank you for choosing" — this is the engagement's paper of record.
- No outcome language, no strategy, no legal analysis anywhere in this message.

## Attorney flags

- Confirm the trust deposit has actually cleared before referencing any dollar
  amount.
- Always attach the executed retainer PDF. A transmittal without the attachment
  defeats the purpose.
- Scope and fee language must be copied from the executed agreement verbatim, not
  summarized.
- Limited-scope and {{SERVICE_NAME}} coaching engagements require different scope language AND
  a different first-7-days timeline. Do not send the full-representation sequence
  on a limited-scope matter.
- Because this master is firm-authored rather than harvested from sent mail, the
  approving attorney should reconcile it against current practice before first
  use.
- Attorney signs. Do not send this one over a staff signature.
