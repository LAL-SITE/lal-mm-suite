# General Motion Drafting Reference

## Petition vs. Motion — Critical Distinction
| Document | When Used |
|---|---|
| Petition | Starts or reopens a case; signed by the client; frames all relief sought |
| Supplemental Petition | Modifies an existing order; treated procedurally like a new petition |
| Motion | Filed within an existing case; makes a specific request to the court |
| Response | Answers a petition or motion filed by the other side |
| Notice | Informs the court or parties of something; does not request relief |
| Proposed Order | Draft order submitted for judge's signature after a hearing or by agreement |

If the user asks for a "motion" but it appears to be a new case or first-time
custody request, stop and clarify — the correct vehicle is a petition.

## Standard Motion Structure
```
[TITLE OF MOTION IN ALL CAPS]

[Petitioner/Respondent], [FULL NAME], by and through undersigned counsel,
respectfully moves this Court for [brief statement of relief] and states:

I. INTRODUCTION (optional for short motions)
One paragraph stating what is being requested and why.

II. BACKGROUND
Numbered paragraphs. Relevant procedural and factual history. Chronological.
Specific. No filler.

III. ARGUMENT / BASIS FOR RELIEF
Numbered paragraphs addressing each ground for the requested relief.
(Do not fabricate legal citations unless training materials provide reliable
authority or attorney directs specific language.)

IV. RELIEF REQUESTED
WHEREFORE, [party] respectfully requests this Court:
1. [Specific item 1]
2. [Specific item 2]
...
And for such other and further relief as this Court deems just and proper.

[SIGNATURE BLOCK]
[CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE]
```

## Common Motions in {{FIRM_NAME}} Practice

### Motion to Compel Mandatory Disclosure
Filed when the opposing party has failed to produce mandatory disclosure documents
within the required deadline. Must identify: (1) what was required and when; (2)
what was not produced; (3) the prejudice caused; (4) the relief sought (production
within X days, sanctions if not complied with).

### Motion for Continuance
Filed when more time is needed for a hearing or deadline. Must state: (1) what
the hearing/deadline is; (2) why more time is needed; (3) that the motion is not
made for delay; (4) the proposed new date or timeline if known.

### Motion to Withdraw
Filed when counsel is withdrawing from representation. Must comply with the
Florida Rules of Professional Conduct. Flag for supervising attorney.

### Motion in Limine
Filed to exclude specific evidence or testimony at trial. Requires attorney
direction — do not draft without specific attorney instruction on what to exclude
and why.

### Notice of Limited Appearance
Used when the firm is representing a client for a limited scope only. Must:
- State the specific scope of representation
- Be filed in the case
- Comply with the local rules on limited representation
A Notice of Termination is required when the limited scope ends.

### Notice of Appearance
Filed in every case where the firm is entering a full appearance. Should be
filed promptly when the firm takes on a case. One of the first things filed.

## Certificate of Service Language
Standard language (e-service via Florida courts e-portal):
```
CERTIFICATE OF SERVICE

I HEREBY CERTIFY that on [DATE], a true and correct copy of the foregoing
was filed with the Clerk of the Court using the Florida Courts E-Filing Portal,
which will serve a copy on all counsel of record and pro se parties registered
with the portal.

                              [ATTORNEY SIGNATURE BLOCK]
```

If serving by mail or hand delivery instead, modify accordingly.

## Proposed Orders
- Draft after a motion is filed and/or a hearing is scheduled
- Mirror the exact relief requested in the motion
- Use the court's preferred order format for the circuit
- Leave signature line for judge; include "ORDERED AND ADJUDGED" language
- Do not include argument or factual narrative — proposed orders are directives only
- Caption must match the pending case exactly
